## Evidence-led context and measurement
Before setting targets, reuse the project's existing product/marketing context: audience and job, pain/objections, differentiation, lawful verbatim customer language, proof and unknowns. Treat code/competitor-derived positioning as a draft for confirmation, not verified customer research. Do not create another competing context file; preserve its existing location/version history.

Tie each prioritized change to an observed problem, hypothesis, baseline, primary business outcome, guardrail and measurement owner. Do not invent traffic, conversion or revenue targets when inputs are missing. For randomized experiments predeclare assignment/exposure units, instrumentation QA, sample/effect assumptions and stopping rules; report effect/uncertainty and guardrails, not only a significance badge. Separate exploratory segments or directional before/after comparisons from causal evidence; inconclusive results remain inconclusive. Search-rank/content changes generally are not visitor-randomized A/B tests, so document seasonality, indexing delay, concurrent changes and attribution limits.

Version decisions and tested learnings with date, evidence/sample, applicability and status; do not turn competitor claims or one anecdote into a proven playbook. Public publishing, tracking edits, ads/outreach and spending still require the appropriate user gates.

## Repeatable reviews only when requested
For an explicitly requested recurring review, define: check cadence, the separate condition to act, one outcome, existing skills used, ordered steps, a source/instrumentation self-check, durable state/idempotency, stop/escalation and output destination. Most valid checks may produce no action. Match cadence to actual signal speed and data volume; do not copy an example interval as a universal rule.

Keep a cursor, handled IDs/cooldowns and in-flight experiments so restarts do not double-act or launch conflicting tests; advance state only after successful persistence and log checked/acted/skipped/error counts. Use internal IDs rather than raw personal data in operational logs. Backfills/resets are explicit dry-run-first work, not permission to contact historical audiences. Proposed learnings and changes to shared business context require review, not silent scheduled rewrites. Publishing, sends, budgets, account changes and destructive operations remain gated; include a stop/disable procedure, but create no schedule by default.
