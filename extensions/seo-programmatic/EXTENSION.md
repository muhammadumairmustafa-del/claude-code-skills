## Research extension: stable imports and first-pattern pilot

For a NEW programmatic pattern, validate the user task before the larger rollout batches above. Start with 5–10 reviewed draft pages whose distinct material delivers the promised asset or answer. Use one stable record/page ID as the import key; keep source and review dates, validate duplicate slugs and overlapping intent, and preview both first import and repeat-update behavior. Separate the writing step from CMS publication. Publishing, external imports and paid tools require their own authorization.

Measure usability, useful original information and relevant business outcomes, not just generated URL count. Indexing and traffic are not guaranteed. The percentage/word-count thresholds elsewhere in this recipe are internal review heuristics, NOT thresholds published by Google and NOT proof of policy compliance. The current official scaled-content policy focuses on pages generated primarily to manipulate rankings without helping users, regardless of how they are created. A working calculator or genuinely useful data view must be judged by task value, not padded to a word count. Re-check official policy before an actual publishing plan.
